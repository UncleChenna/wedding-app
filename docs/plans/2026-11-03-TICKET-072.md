# TICKET-072 plan

Owner unclechenna (claude); branch TICKET-072 from origin/develop `0e6247c`, after TICKET-071 (#82) merged. TICKET-071 is
done; no one else holds ordering submission work.

## Outcome

A guest at a confirmed table builds an order from the menu (quantities and a 140-character note) and sends it. A bad
connection retries automatically and says so; a lost response never creates a second order. The page shows sending,
sent ("On its way. Sit back, we've got this.") and failed-with-retry states.

## Approach

- Idempotency uses the existing unique `orders.client_request_id`; no migration. A replay is compared with the stored
  order (table, note and items in any order): identical means the original order, different means a 409 mismatch.
- `POST /api/orders` (JSON only, 8 KB cap, Zod) calls `createOrder`, which in one transaction checks the table, locks the
  chosen menu rows `FOR SHARE`, checks availability and `max_per_order`, and inserts the order, its station rows and
  items. A racing duplicate loses the unique insert and answers as a replay.
- `submitOrder` retries transport failures, timeouts, 408/429 and 5xx with 1s/2s/4s backoff and the same id.
- The TICKET-071 menu becomes controlled: `useMenuAvailability` (polling), `MenuSections` (display) and
  `OrderComposer` (cart, note, sending states).

## Scope boundary

The schema (TICKET-171) only stores an item under its station's `station_orders` row, so writing an order necessarily
creates one row per station. TICKET-074 still owns its tests and the guest confirmation grouped by station. Limits and
cooldown are TICKET-073; live status is TICKET-075; opening and closing ordering is TICKET-080.

## Verification

intercepting the network. Screenshots, axe and overflow in every theme at 375/768/1280. Lighthouse and `pnpm verify`.
