Implement a pure, dependency-free phone normalizer for INV-3 sharing. Return a discriminated success/error result for unknown input; never coerce numbers (leading zeroes may already be lost). Support UK national numbers, bare 44, explicit + and 00 international prefixes, presentation spaces/hyphens/dots/parentheses, and explicit UK (0). Reject extensions, malformed punctuation, unsupported ambiguous local formats and implausible lengths.

This validates contact-number structure, not allocation, ownership, reachability or WhatsApp registration. International contacts require 7–15 digits; UK national numbers require 10 digits, or 9 for geographic numbers beginning 1. Preserve significant international zeroes. No dependency, schema or UI changes.

Tests: table-driven examples, invalid values and runtime types, boundaries, optional trunk zero, international preservation and idempotence. Run tests before implementation, then quick/full gates. Reviewable PR to develop; detailed evidence and handoff. References: PRD INV-3; [ITU E.164](https://www.itu.int/rec/T-REC-E.164/en) for the 15-digit maximum.
